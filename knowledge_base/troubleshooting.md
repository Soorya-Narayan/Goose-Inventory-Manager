# Troubleshooting Guide

## Common Alarms & Solutions

### 1. Low Flow Rate Alarm
**Description**: Flow rate has dropped below the setpoint (e.g., < 100 L/min) for more than 10 seconds.
**Possible Causes**:
-   Pump cavitation (air in line).
-   Clogged strainer or filter.
-   Valve failed to open completely.
-   Leak in supply line.
**Troubleshooting Steps**:
1.  Check the **Supply Pump (P-101)** status. Is it running?
2.  Inspect the **Line Strainer**. Clean if necessary.
3.  Verify **Flow Control Valve (FCV-101)** position.
4.  Check for leaks in the suction side of the pump.

### 2. Temperature Deviation
**Description**: Temperature is outside the acceptable range (Setpoint ± 5°C).
**Possible Causes**:
-   Heating element failure.
-   Steam valve stuck closed.
-   Temp sensor (TT-101) malfunction.
**Troubleshooting Steps**:
1.  Check **Steam Valve** position. Ensure steam supply is available.
2.  Verify **Temperature Sensor** reading against local gauge.
3.  If temperature is too high, check if **Cooling Valve** is opening.

### 3. Conductivity Low (Chemical Concentration)
**Description**: Chemical concentration is below target (e.g., < 20 mS/cm for Caustic).
**Possible Causes**:
-   Dosing pump failure.
-   Empty chemical drum.
-   Dilution due to water valve leak.
**Troubleshooting Steps**:
1.  Check **Chemical Dosing Pump** operation.
2.  Inspect chemical drum level. Refill if empty.
3.  Check **Water Supply Valve** for internal leaking.

## Maintenance Checks
-   **Daily**: Inspect pumps for leaks. Check chemical levels.
-   **Weekly**: Calibrate pH and Conductivity sensors.
-   **Monthly**: Inspect valve seals and gaskets.
