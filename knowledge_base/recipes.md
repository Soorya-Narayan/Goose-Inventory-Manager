# Standard Recipes

## 1. 3-Step Caustic Wash (Standard)
**Goal**: Remove organic soils (fats, proteins).
**Steps**:
1.  **Pre-Rinse**: 
    -   Source: Recovery Tank / Fresh Water
    -   Duration: 5 min
    -   Temp: Ambient
    -   Goal: Flush loose soil.
2.  **Caustic Wash**:
    -   Source: Tank A
    -   Concentration: 2.0 - 2.5% NaOH
    -   Duration: 20 min
    -   Temp: 80°C
    -   Goal: Saponify fats and dissolve proteins.
3.  **Final Rinse**:
    -   Source: Fresh Water
    -   Duration: 10 min
    -   Temp: Ambient
    -   Goal: Remove all chemical residues.

## 2. 5-Step Acid Sanitize (Heavy Duty)
**Goal**: Remove mineral scales and sanitize.
**Steps**:
1.  **Pre-Rinse**: 3 min, Ambient.
2.  **Caustic Wash**: 15 min, 80°C, 2.0%.
3.  **Intermediate Rinse**: 5 min, Ambient.
4.  **Acid Wash**:
    -   Source: Tank B
    -   Concentration: 1.0 - 1.5% HNO3
    -   Duration: 10 min
    -   Temp: 60°C
    -   Goal: Remove mineral stone/scale.
5.  **Final Rinse**: 10 min, Ambient.

## 3. Sterilize (Hot Water)
**Goal**: Thermal disinfection.
**Steps**:
1.  **Hot Water Circulate**:
    -   Source: Fresh Water (Heated)
    -   Temp: > 90°C
    -   Duration: 20 min
