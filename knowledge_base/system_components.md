# System Components

## Tanks
-   **Tank A (Caustic)**: Holds 2% NaOH solution at 80°C. Insulated. Capacity: 2000L.
-   **Tank B (Acid)**: Holds 1.5% HNO3 solution at 65°C. Capacity: 2000L.
-   **Tank C (Rinse Water)**: Holds fresh water for intermediate and final rinses. Ambient Temp. Capacity: 2000L.
-   **Tank D (Recovery)**: Captures final rinse water for reuse as pre-rinse in the next cycle. Capacity: 2000L.

## Pumps
-   **Supply Pump (P-101)**: Centrifugal pump, 5.5 kW. 
    -   Function: Delivers fluid to the process circuits.
    -   Nominal Flow: 300 L/min.
-   **Return Pump (P-102)**: Scavenge pump to return fluid to CIP station.
    -   Function: Ensures no fluid buildup in the circuit.
-   **Dosing Pumps (P-201/202)**:
    -   P-201: Caustic Dosing.
    -   P-202: Acid Dosing.

## Valves
-   **Flow Control Valve (FCV-101)**: Modulating valve to regulate flow rate.
-   **Steam Valve (TCV-101)**: Regulates steam flow to the heat exchanger.
-   **Circuit Valves (XV-301/302)**: Diverter valves to select cleaning path (Circuit A vs Circuit B).

## Sensors
-   **Flow Meter (FIT-101)**: Magnetic flow meter. Output: 4-20mA.
-   **Temperature Transmitter (TT-101)**: RTD Pt100.
-   **Conductivity Transmitter (AIT-101)**: Inductive conductivity sensor. Used to determine chemical concentration and phase separation.
