A modern web platform that connects to factory PLCs, visualizes Clean-In-Place process data in real-time, and uses AI diagnostics to predict failures before they occur.

🏭
What is CIP?
Automated in-place cleaning of food, pharma & dairy equipment — without disassembly. Requires precise control of temperature, flow, chemicals & pressure.
📊
What does it do?
Reads PLC tags in real-time, displays live KPIs, tracks each CIP cycle step-by-step, manages alarms, and generates compliance reports automatically.
🧠
What makes it different?
7 purpose-built AI models run continuously on live sensor data — predicting failures, detecting drift & optimizing recipes. No SCADA system can match this.
